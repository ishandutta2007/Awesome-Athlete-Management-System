# Awesome-Athlete-Management-System

## Top Athlete Management System Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Athlete Monitoring, Training Load, Performance Data, Injury Risk, Wellness Surveys & Integrated Sports Science Platforms*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Athlete Management Systems (AMS)**. These platforms centralize performance, medical, wellness, and training-load data so coaches, sports scientists, and medical staff can monitor athletes, reduce injury risk, personalize programs, and support return-to-play decisions.



**Examples** include Smartabase (Teamworks), Kinduct, Kitman Labs, Edge10, AMP by Catapult, Teamworks, Presagia Sports, BridgeAthletic, CoachMePlus, and Fusion Sport (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured commercial AMS platforms with deep integrations to wearables, medical systems, and multi-disciplinary workflows dominate elite sport. Open-source options are more limited but include **REGMON** and smaller research/athlete-tracking projects. This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Smartabase (Teamworks)](https://teamworks.com/smartabase)**  

  Leading athlete management system for integrated performance teams—centralizing load, testing, nutrition, medical, and survey data with strong analytics and integrations.



- **[Kitman Labs](https://www.kitmanlabs.com/)**  

  Intelligence platform for athlete availability, injury risk, and performance decision support used across professional and elite sport.



- **[Catapult AMP / Catapult ecosystem](https://www.catapult.com/)**  

  Athlete monitoring and performance platform tightly linked with Catapult’s wearable and tracking technology.



- **[Kinduct, Edge10, Presagia Sports](https://www.kinduct.com/)**  

  Athlete data management and performance platforms supporting multi-disciplinary staff workflows and longitudinal athlete profiles.



- **[Teamworks, BridgeAthletic, CoachMePlus, Fusion Sport](https://teamworks.com/)**  

  Broader sports operations and training platforms that include athlete management, strength & conditioning, wellness, and team communication features.



- **[Other commercial athlete monitoring & AMS platforms](https://teamworks.com/)**  

  Solutions focused on training load, wellness questionnaires, medical records, and performance analytics for teams and organizations.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[REGMON](https://github.com/REGmon-project/regmon)**  

  Open-source web application for athlete monitoring in sport practice and research. Supports customizable forms, dashboards, analysis templates, and permission-aware data sharing between athletes, coaches, and staff (GDPR-oriented design).



- **[Open athletics / training dashboards](https://github.com/search?q=athlete+monitoring+OR+training+load+OR+PMC)**  

  Community projects that generate Performance Management Charts (PMC), training-load visualizations, and volume tracking from wearable or manual data sources.



- **[Strava-connected open dashboards](https://github.com/MenachoIvan/open-athletics)**  

  Open-source performance dashboards that pull training data (e.g., from Strava) for volume and efficiency tracking with a clean, athlete-focused UI.



- **[Sports science calculation libraries](https://github.com/search?q=training+load+OR+Banister+OR+TRIMP+OR+ACWR)**  

  Open implementations of common training-load metrics (TRIMP, ACWR, Banister models, etc.) that can underpin custom monitoring tools.



- **[Athlete / team management prototypes](https://github.com/search?q=athlete+management+OR+sports+performance+platform)**  

  Smaller or regional open projects covering performance logging, injury tracking, and coach–athlete communication.



- **[Wellness & questionnaire open tools](https://github.com/search?q=wellness+questionnaire+OR+RPE+open+source)**  

  Simple form and survey systems adapted for daily wellness, RPE, and readiness collection.



- **[Wearable data open pipelines](https://github.com/search?q=Garmin+OR+Polar+OR+wearable+data+open+source)**  

  Tools for ingesting and processing data from common sports wearables that feed athlete monitoring workflows.



- **[Research & academic AMS frameworks](https://github.com/search?q=athlete+monitoring+research+OR+sports+science+platform)**  

  University and research-group platforms designed for longitudinal athlete studies and GDPR-compliant data management.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **REGMON**: The most complete open-source athlete monitoring application currently available for practice and research use.

- **Training-load & PMC tools**: Open calculators and dashboards for classic sports-science metrics.

- **Wearable-to-dashboard pipelines**: Self-hosted ingestion + visualization for teams that already own their data.

- **Composable stacks**: Forms (wellness/RPE) + time-series database + dashboards + simple permission model.

- Full commercial AMS platforms remain far ahead in integrations, medical modules, multi-disciplinary workflows, and elite-sport support.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

**REGMON** is the strongest open-source starting point for structured athlete monitoring with customizable forms and dashboards.  

Beyond that, teams can combine open training-load libraries, wearable data pipelines, and general form/dashboard tools.  

Commercial platforms (Smartabase/Teamworks, Kitman Labs, Catapult AMP, Kinduct, etc.) provide deep integrations with GPS/wearables, medical systems, nutrition, and multi-staff workflows required by professional teams.  

Amateur, research, or resource-constrained programs may succeed with open or hybrid stacks; elite and professional environments typically rely on commercial AMS platforms, sometimes exporting data into open analytics environments.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Athlete management systems handle sensitive health, performance, and sometimes medical data. Ensure appropriate privacy, consent, security, and (where applicable) sports-medicine regulatory compliance.

- Open-source tools offer transparency and data ownership but require technical capacity for hosting, validation, and integration with wearables or medical processes. Commercial platforms shift operational and support burden to the vendor. Choose according to competition level, staff expertise, and data-governance requirements.
